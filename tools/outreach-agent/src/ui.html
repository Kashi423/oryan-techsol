Safety limits

Emails per day. Start at 10 with a new mailbox and raise slowly. Sends only Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:00 on this computer.

Services & starting prices

Emails lead with the one service that fits each business, and every email lists all of your services in the signature. If you enter a starting price, the email quotes it as “from $X” for that service. Leave a price blank and no price is mentioned. The agent never says it is “cheaper than the market”, because that cannot be proven.

Real proof points optional

One per line, only TRUE statements about work you have really done, for example “We built an online booking site for a dental clinic in Lahore”. Emails use at most one, word for word. Leave empty and the agent claims no past work. (The case studies and testimonials on your website are samples, so they are never used.)

Email look

Research on cold email favours plain text for the first message: it reads as personal and reaches the inbox more often. “Auto” sends plain text for first emails and follow-ups, and your branded layout once someone has replied. Branded HTML is a light layout with your colours, signature and an unsubscribe line. There are no tracking pixels either way.
