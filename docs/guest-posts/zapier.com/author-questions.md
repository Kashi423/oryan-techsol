# Questions for Waqas (answer in your own words; these become the article)

Rules: real events only. No client names, numbers or results unless the client agrees. If you cannot answer Q1-Q4, do not pitch Option 1 (see pitch.md). Zapier forbids AI-generated writing, so write your answers and the final text yourself.

Option 1 (Zap strain)
1. Describe one real workflow you built or inherited in Zapier or Make that started to strain. What business was it for (type only), what did it do, and how many times a week or month did it run?
2. What went wrong, specifically? Cost, errors, slowness, missing data, a limit you hit? What did it cost the client in time or money, if you know?
3. Which of these signs have you actually seen: needing memory of earlier runs, looping over many records, heavy or long-running work, high cost of failure, data that had to stay in the client's own systems? Give one example for each you have seen.
4. Before writing code, what did you try inside Zapier or Make (splitting the Zap, a Code step, error handlers, replay)? What worked, what did not?
5. What rule do you use to decide fix, split or replace? Have you ever been wrong, and what happened?
6. When you replaced part of a workflow with code, what did you build (language, where it ran) and what stayed in Zapier or Make?
7. How did you switch over? Did you run old and new in parallel, for how long, and what differences showed up?
8. What would you do differently on that project? What did you cut, and what broke?

Option 2 (exceptions page)
9. Describe one mapping session with a client. Who did you talk to, and what exception surfaced that nobody had written down? What did you do about it (path, handoff, or left manual)?

Handover (Option 3 material)
10. Have you handed an automation to a client's staff? What did you write down for them, and did an alert or fallback ever save the day or fail?

About you (for the bio and "topic authority")
11. Which tools have you personally used for automation (Zapier, Make, n8n, custom code), for how long, and roughly for how many clients or projects? Only state what you can defend.
12. Which pieces have you written yourself that can serve as writing samples (links)? And what is your LinkedIn URL?

Permissions
13. May you describe any of these projects publicly? If not, we keep them anonymous ("a clinic", "a retailer") or leave them out.
