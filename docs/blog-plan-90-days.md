# 90-day blog plan (2026-11-08 → 2027-02-05)

The first 30 scheduled articles run 2026-10-09 → 2026-11-07 (already written). This plan covers the next 90 days, one article a day.

## How to use it

- Each row: publish date, working title, primary keyword, category and the FAQs people actually ask (taken from the question styles of Google "People also ask", Reddit, Quora, YouTube comments and AI assistants). Put 5 FAQs on each article: these 3 plus 2 more from the article itself. They become the on-page FAQ section and the FAQPage structured data.
- Rules for every article: ~1,500+ words, one primary keyword in title/H1/first 100 words/URL, 4–6 internal links to older posts only, one infographic block at least, 5 FAQs, a service CTA, a cover photo with descriptive alt text (Pixabay, self-hosted WebP 1200×675).
- Image SEO: file name = slug, descriptive alt "Cover image for <short title>", width/height set, image sitemap entry, ImageObject in BlogPosting schema, 1200×630 OG image. Create one extra original infographic image per article where possible (Google Images traffic).
- Refresh: every 90 days update the oldest 10 posts (new stats, new FAQs) and bump the `updated` date.
- Measure weekly in Search Console: impressions, queries, pages indexed, image search tab. Double down on topics that already get impressions.
